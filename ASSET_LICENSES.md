# Packed ground material assets

The ornamental planting beds use **Leafy Grass** by Charlotte Baglioni, from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass). The downloaded 2K JPEG diffuse, roughness and OpenGL normal maps are in `textures/leafy_grass/` and packed into the Blender file when the botanical refinement is applied.

These assets use [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license), permitting use and redistribution including commercial work. Metadata from the public API is preserved in `references/polyhaven_leafy_grass_files.json`; all three downloads matched the published file sizes and MD5 hashes on 30 September 2026.

The lawn uses **Grass 004** from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass004), also CC0. This is a procedural PBR material with a published 1.4m repeat, rather than a site photograph. The original `textures/Grass004_2K-JPG.zip` passed an archive CRC check; the extracted colour, roughness and OpenGL normal maps are in `textures/Grass004/` and packed into Blender when the ground refinement is applied.

This is a general photographic ground material beneath planting beds, rather than a photograph or scan from Surat Diamond Bourse. Architectural reference photographs remain research material with their original rights; they are not used as textures on the model.
