# Surat Diamond Bourse — reconstruction references

This model is a presentation reconstruction. Source material establishes the overall arrangement and recurring character; it does not provide a complete construction drawing set. Dimensions marked as approximations below must remain editable in the model.

## Primary project sources

- [Official SDB construction-details circular](https://www.suratdiamondbourse.in/upload/image/circular/16-SDB-%20ENg%20%26%20Guj%20-%20Constraction%20Cost%20%26%20Details.pdf): G+15 offices, 3.9 m typical floor-to-floor height, 6.4 m ground-floor height, a spine at least 24 ft wide that widens at both ends, nine towers joined at every floor, 15 acres of landscape, and over 60,000 ft² landscape between a pair of towers.
- [Morphogenesis project page](https://www.morphogenesis.org/projects/surat-diamond-bourse/): architect source and project photographs.
- [Architect site plan](https://images.adsttc.com/media/images/656f/5470/f84b/cc06/ab19/cef8/large_jpg/site-plan-3.jpg?1701794948): scale bar, 4 north-side and 5 south-side tower positions, long central spine, Diamond Club, utility building, courtyards and perimeter circulation.
- [Architect typical floor plan](https://images.adsttc.com/media/images/656f/546f/f84b/cc06/ab19/cef7/large_jpg/typical-floor-plan-6.jpg?1701794956): stepped thin-slab tower character, the spine and its connections.
- [Architect text and credited photos](https://www.archdaily.com/1010709/surat-diamond-bourse-morphogenesis): north–south office-tower orientation, central-axis concept, shaded courts, materials and circulation description.
- [Morphogenesis/FuturArc project data](https://www.morphogenesis.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/FuturArc_Dec-2018_SDB_compressed.pdf): project/site area and published height datum.

The reference images themselves are excluded from this public release. They are not required by the Blender scene and are not included as model textures. Photograph credits: Morphogenesis / Edmund Sumner; PTI aerial via Hindustan Times.

## Modelling assumptions

- The plan scale yields an approximately 350 m long central spine and a roughly 370 × 200 m office/spine assembly. These are plan-derived estimates, not construction dimensions.
- Tower rows use a 4 + 5 stagger: ordinary anchor spacings are roughly 60–65 m; the north row is shifted about 20–25 m along the spine. The non-repeating eastern terminals are deliberately larger.
- Typical towers are treated as 14–18 m wide stepped slabs reaching roughly 85–95 m away from the spine. Their midpoints step laterally by about 35% of each tower width. The exact tower footprints are inferred from the published plan.
- The public 81.9 m figure is listed in the official SDB circular as above mean sea level. The model uses roughly 70–72 m from local grade to roofline, consistent with G+15 and the published floor pitches, pending a surveyed elevation.
- The public plan does not scale to the full published 35.3–35.5 acre property; the landscape/site boundary is therefore designed as a representative context around the building assembly, not as a cadastral survey.
